Play Store is strict about this. Answer exactly:

### Section A — Data collection & security
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **Yes** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | **Yes** *(HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ enforced everywhere)* |
