## 1. LinkedIn — Founder Story (pinned post)

Pin this to your profile. It is your introduction to everyone — investors, solicitors, partners, customers.

---

**Why I built AI Advocate.**

My wife lives with me in the UK on a spouse visa. She is a refugee, and her French travel document expired while she was here. The renewal had been filed; only the trip back to France to collect it remained.

We followed every proper channel. We instructed UK immigration solicitors — they couldn't help. We instructed French immigration solicitors based in France — they couldn't help either. The only advice we received was, "Go to the French embassy in London. She is under their protection, they will know what to do."

We booked the appointment. We arrived on the day. We were met at the door by non-French security guards who turned her away. She never reached the official she had paid to see, at the one institution that is supposed to protect her.

She felt invisible. We had paid solicitors. We had followed every official process. There was no one left to ask.

I work at an airport, so I approached two airlines directly. One would let her fly but couldn't promise what French border control would say on arrival. The national carrier of the country protecting her refused her boarding outright.

After weeks of research I found a single untested route: Eurostar. The French border begins on UK soil at St Pancras — meaning if she were refused, she would still be in the UK, not stranded abroad. It worked. She reached France, renewed her travel document, and came home.

That experience changed me. There are people in that same position right now — frightened, running out of money, running out of doors to knock on. People who can't afford £350-an-hour solicitors for an answer that may or may not be the right one.

So I built **AI Advocate**: real-time legal information grounded in UK statute, available in 11 languages, free to start, with a Pro tier at £19.99/month for those who need ongoing support. Built deliberately for the person who has been turned away one too many times.

If anyone in your network is working with vulnerable clients — immigration, family, employment, debt, tenancy — I would genuinely value the introduction.

🔗 https://aiadvocate.co.uk

## 4. Posting cadence on LinkedIn

| Day | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Founder story (pinned, post once, leave forever) | Hook |
| **Tuesday** | A real legal myth Lex helped someone debunk last week | Proof of value |
| **Wednesday** | A solicitor partner shoutout (with their permission) | Social proof |

| **Friday** | Week-recap status update (template above) | Momentum |
| **Sat/Sun** | Engage on others' posts — don't post your own | Algorithm tip |

The LinkedIn algorithm rewards comments more than posts. Spend 20 minutes a day commenting thoughtfully on UK legal / immigration / employment-law posts. Your founder-story post will then surface in those readers' feeds when they click your profile.
