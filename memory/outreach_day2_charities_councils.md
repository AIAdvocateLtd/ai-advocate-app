- **Don't exceed 20 emails/hour** — Gmail throttles cold sends. Spread across the day or use Gmail's "Schedule send".

- **Subject line A/B test:** I've used "15-min idea?" for half the councils and "partnership idea" for the other half — log open rates if your inbox supports it (Mixmax, Yesware, or Gmail "Read receipt" on a per-send basis).
- **Follow-up date: 01/07** for everyone (Day 8 from today).
- If anyone says "not interested" / "please remove" → mark Closed-Declined and DO NOT follow up. Burning the relationship is worse than no reply.
