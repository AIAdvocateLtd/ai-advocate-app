## 🛡 Critical legal reminder

Don't promise specific commission terms in cold emails beyond what's in the Partner Agreement. The cold email is **lead generation**, not a contract. The Partner Agreement is the contract — and it must be signed by both parties **before** any referral code is issued.

If anyone replies asking for higher rates or longer terms — **say no, politely**. Consistency is what protects you when partner #15 hears that partner #3 got a special deal.
