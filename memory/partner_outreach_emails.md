## 📝 General sending guidance

1. **Don't blast send all four templates in one day.** Send 3-5 per day with personalised opening lines.
2. **Track responses in your /founder diary** as you go — paste the diary entry "Sent to X today" for an audit trail.
3. **If anyone replies "interested" — don't reply with terms.** Reply with: "Brilliant. I'll send the formal Partner Agreement now and we can discuss next steps."
4. **Reuse the same PDF Partner Agreement** every time. Don't tweak the cap or rate for one partner — that becomes a precedent.
5. **First 5 partners that sign get a "Founding Partner" badge** in your dashboard — a nice incentive that costs you nothing.

---

## 🛡 Critical legal reminder

Don't promise specific commission terms in cold emails beyond what's in the Partner Agreement. The cold email is **lead generation**, not a contract. The Partner Agreement is the contract — and it must be signed by both parties **before** any referral code is issued.

If anyone replies asking for higher rates or longer terms — **say no, politely**. Consistency is what protects you when partner #15 hears that partner #3 got a special deal.
