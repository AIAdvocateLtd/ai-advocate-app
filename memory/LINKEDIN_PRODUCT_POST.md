## 🚦 Posting strategy (this is the bit that matters most)

A LinkedIn post lives or dies in the first 60 minutes. Do these in order:

1. **Post on a Tuesday or Wednesday between 8–10am UK time.** Highest professional engagement.
2. **Put `aiadvocate.co.uk` in the FIRST COMMENT, not in the body.** LinkedIn penalises posts with outbound links — putting the link in a comment instead can 3x reach.
3. **Within 5 minutes of posting**, like and comment yourself with a "thank you for reading" + the link.
4. **Tag 2-3 people who would genuinely care** (solicitors you know, friends in legal-tech, the founder of any UK access-to-justice charity). Don't tag random influencers — LinkedIn detects that.
5. **For the next hour**, reply to every comment you receive within 10 minutes. The algorithm rewards engaged conversations.
6. **DO NOT delete and re-post if reach is low.** Each post compounds your profile authority over time.
