# Founding Firms Outreach Kit

> The goal: hand-pick **20 UK law firms** across employment, family, immigration, property and consumer-rights practices, and offer each a **90-day free Premium tier** in exchange for a testimonial and being listed in the directory at launch.
>
> Owner of this initiative: founder.
> Target: 20 firms confirmed before TestFlight goes external.
> Estimated effort: 2-3 hours of outreach + 1-2 follow-up emails per firm.

---

## 🎯 Why this matters for launch

1. **Empty directory = dead app.** When the first user opens the lawyer-finder, seeing 0 or 3 listings sends a "this is unfinished" signal. 20 listings across major UK cities sends "this is serious."
2. **Founding firms become reference customers.** Their testimonials go on the marketing site, the App Store description, and into PR pitches.
3. **B2B revenue trajectory.** Even at 0% conversion at end of trial, those 20 firms recommend you to 5+ others each. Network effect within the SRA-regulated community is brutal.
4. **You set the floor on Premium pricing.** £199/mo locked in for the founding cohort means future firms upgrading from Featured see "I could get Premium for less if I'd joined earlier" — incentive to subscribe at the published rate.

---

## 📋 Step-by-step

1. **Identify** 20 candidate firms from the target list (below) or your own network. Mix of cities + practice areas.
2. **Send the cold email** (template below) from your personal/founder email. Aim for partner / managing solicitor / business-development director.
3. **Wait 48 hours** — if no response, send the follow-up.
4. **Onboarding call** (15-30 min Zoom): show the firm portal, explain the trial mechanics.
5. **Issue the comp**: log into AI Advocate as admin, open Settings → 🏛 Firm trial → search firm → grant **90 days Premium**, reason "Founding Firm cohort".
6. **Send onboarding email** with their portal login link.
7. **Day 60**: check-in email — ask what's working, gather a testimonial.
8. **Day 80**: conversion email — "We hope you've seen the value, here's how to lock in £199/mo before your trial expires day 90."

## 📈 Expected conversion math

If you cold-email 20 firms:
- 50% reply (10 firms)
- 70% of replies agree to the trial (7 firms onboarded)
- 40% of trials convert to paid Premium (3 firms × £199/mo = £597 MRR)

That's £7,164/year of confirmed B2B MRR from 2 hours of outreach. Each converted firm typically refers 1-2 others within their network within 6 months — so the **real 12-month value** is closer to **£12,000-£15,000 MRR**.

Combined with consumer subscriptions, this is your fastest path to break-even.
