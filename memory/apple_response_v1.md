**2. App purpose & target audience**
AI Advocate is a legal-information assistant for UK consumers who cannot afford a solicitor or need to quickly understand their rights. It solves the access-to-justice gap: over 3.6 million people in England & Wales face a legal issue each year without legal help (Law Society, 2023).
Target audience: UK residents aged 18+ facing employment, housing, family, consumer, or immigration issues. Available in 11 languages to serve non-native English speakers.
Value: instant plain-English legal information (free tier), letter drafting, case file organisation, and signposting to free Legal Aid advisers. Explicitly NOT a substitute for a solicitor.

**5. Regional differences**
The app functions consistently across all regions. Users can select any of 15 supported countries; Lex adapts to that country's legal framework, but the app is primarily trained on UK law and displays a warning modal for users outside the UK advising them to verify with a local qualified lawyer. Pricing, features, and account deletion are identical worldwide. Not restricted to any region.

**6. Regulated industry**
AI Advocate operates outside "reserved legal activities" under the UK Legal Services Act 2007. Specifically:
- AI Advocate is NOT a law firm and is NOT SRA regulated. This is stated in Terms of Service, in the App Store description, on every AI chat response as a persistent footer ("AI Advocate is not a law firm and is not SRA regulated. AI-generated legal information, not legal advice"), and as an italic tag under each AI response ("This is legal information, not legal advice").
- We do not conduct any reserved legal activity (advocacy, litigation, reserved instrument, probate, notarial, oaths).
- We provide general legal information only — no solicitor-client relationship is created.
- All references to solicitor services signpost users to independent regulated professionals or free services (Citizens Advice, Legal Aid Agency, LawWorks).
- Payments handled by Stripe (a licensed PSP); AI Advocate performs no regulated financial activity.

No additional authorisation is required to publish an AI legal-information app in the UK, per SRA guidance: https://www.sra.org.uk/consumers/using-solicitor/unregulated-legal-services/
