An AI-powered legal-information app helping ordinary people understand their rights — and connecting them with regulated solicitors when they need real representation. We're inviting 20 UK firms to launch with us as Founding Premium partners.

The opportunity

Most UK adults can't afford £300/hour solicitor fees, so they Google. They land on a mix of unverified forums, AI hallucinations, and out-of-date Citizens Advice pages. The conversion path from "I have a problem" to "I instruct a solicitor" is broken.

AI Advocate fixes the discovery layer. We give people genuine sourced legal information grounded in legislation.gov.uk and BAILII — and when their issue needs a real lawyer, we route them to you.
