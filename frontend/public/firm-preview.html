Three options for the top-tier visual signature. Pick one (or mix).

Option A · Moving Shimmer

Static logo. A subtle gold "shine" sweeps across diagonally every few seconds — like polished gold catching the light. Tasteful, premium, never distracting.

Option B · 3D Spin

The logo gently rotates on its Y-axis. High-impact, eye-catching, but can feel busy if it runs constantly. Best used as an entrance animation that stops after 1-2 spins.

Option C · Sparkle + Glow

Pulsing gold halo around the logo with small white sparkle particles popping at random points. Most "magical" — strongest premium feel, perfect for the Premium Partner badge.
