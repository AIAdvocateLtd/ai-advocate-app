Part 2 · Floating Logo Treatments (v2)

No box behind the logo — it floats freely in space. Four options below.

Option A · Hover Float

Logo gently rises and falls. The gold pool of light beneath it tightens and dims as the logo lifts — like a coin levitating. Subtle, classy, never distracting.

Option B · 3D Tilt Sway

Logo tilts left ↔ right on its vertical axis (max ~22°) with a tiny lift — like a hovering medallion catching the light from different angles. Strong 3D feel, no full spin.

Option C · Float + Shimmer

Logo floats up & down while a diagonal gold shine sweeps across it every ~3s — like polished gold catching the sun. Premium & refined; works great as an always-on signature.

Option D · Floating Sparkles

Logo floats with a pulsing gold halo and small white sparkles popping at random points around it. Most "magical" — best for the Premium Partner badge and celebration moments.
