Frequently asked questions

Is AI Advocate a real lawyer?

No. AI Advocate is a legal-information assistant — not a law firm and not regulated by the SRA. It gives you instant, plain-English information about UK law and helps you draft letters and organise your case, but it is not a substitute for regulated legal advice from a qualified solicitor. For complex matters, we signpost you to Citizens Advice, Legal Aid Agency or an independent solicitor.

How much does AI Advocate cost?

Free is £0 forever with 2 free Lex questions, no card required. Plus is £19.99/month for 300 Lex messages a month, 20 letters, Contract Review and Court Prep. Pro adds 600 Lex messages a month. Pro is £34.99/month, or £319.99/year (save £100), for everything in Plus plus Deep Think for complex cases, hearing notes and transcription, and priority legal research. One-off packs are also available from £4.99. Prices shown are the total you pay. Cancel any time.

What areas of UK law does AI Advocate cover?

Employment (unfair dismissal, discrimination, wages), housing (deposits, evictions, disrepair), family (divorce, child arrangements), consumer (refunds, faulty goods), small claims, debt, immigration, welfare benefits, personal injury basics, will drafting basics, and neighbour disputes. Primary training is England & Wales law with Scotland and Northern Ireland variations flagged where relevant.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. Chat messages and case files are encrypted at rest. Lex Vault items are encrypted client-side with a PIN only you know — we cannot read them. We store data in EU-West MongoDB Atlas (UK-GDPR compliant). We never sell data to advertisers or data brokers. You can export or delete your data any time via Settings → Manage My Data.

What languages does AI Advocate support?

11 languages: English (UK), Spanish, French, Arabic, Polish, German, Hindi, Urdu, Italian, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese. Lex understands questions in any supported language and can reply in the same language — legal terms are automatically kept in the original English legal term with a translated explanation.

Do I need to be in the UK to use AI Advocate?

The app is primarily trained on UK law. You can use it from anywhere in the world, but for jurisdictions outside the UK we display a warning that legal answers may not fully apply to your country. If you need advice on non-UK matters, we recommend consulting a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.
