Logo Shimmer Options (transparent — no box)

Option 0 · No shimmer

Plain static logo as a baseline for comparison. No animation at all.

Option 1 · Soft Glow Pulse

A warm gold halo gently fades in and out around the logo every 3.5s. Logo itself stays the same brightness. Subtle.

Option 2 · Brightness Pulse

The gold itself brightens and saturates briefly — like the logo catches the light. Now on transparent PNG so no box appears.

Option 3 · Sparkle Particles

Tiny white sparkles pop at random points around the logo. Logo is completely static — only sparkles move. Magical feel.

Option 4 · Slow Breath

The logo softly breathes (scales 1.00 ↔ 1.04) while the gold glow underneath swells. Most "alive" — like a slow heartbeat.

Option 5 · Hover Float

Logo floats up and down ~10px with a gentle shadow beneath. No flash, no shine — just elegant levitation.
