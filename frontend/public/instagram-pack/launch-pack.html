🛠 Recommended workflow

  1. Canva (free) — drag a screenshot onto a 1080 × 1350 dark gold template, add the caption text, export. ~30s per post.
  2. Use the AI Advocate brand colours: gold #f7c948, deep gold #d4af37, black #0a0a0a.
  3. CapCut (free) for Reels — string 5-6 screenshots together with quick zooms + trending audio.
  4. Meta Business Suite — schedule everything the night before. Add hashtags in the first comment (cleaner caption, same reach).
