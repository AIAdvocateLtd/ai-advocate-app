## 📐 Image sizes Instagram needs

| Format | Size | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Square feed | 1080 × 1080 | Most feed posts |
| Vertical feed (4:5) | 1080 × 1350 | Premium-looking feed |
| Story / Reel | 1080 × 1920 | Stories, Reels |
| Carousel | 1080 × 1080 each | Slideshow posts |

The iPhone screenshots are captured at **393 × 852 @ 3x = 1179 × 2556 native px**. They fit Reel format beautifully (just centre + add black bars or text-overlay top/bottom in Canva). For square feed posts, drop the screenshot onto a dark-gold background in Canva.

## 🛠 Tooling recommendation

Easiest workflow:
1. **Canva** (free) → drag a screenshot in, drop on a 1080×1350 dark gold template, add caption text on top — done in 30s per post.
2. Use the same gold (#f7c948) + black (#0a0a0a) palette as the app for instant brand recognition.
3. **CapCut** (free) for Reels — string 5-6 screenshots with quick zooms + the trending audio.

## 🚀 Pro tip

Schedule everything via **Meta Business Suite** (free, native) the night before. Tag every post with location "London, United Kingdom" and add #LegalTech #UKLaw to the first comment (cleaner caption, same reach).
