How to appeal a parking fine in the UK

Found a ticket on your windscreen or a letter in the post? First work out who sent it, because the rules for council and private tickets are completely different.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · Mainly covers England and Wales
Quick answer: a Penalty Charge Notice (PCN) comes from a council. Challenge it within 14 days to keep the 50% discount, then appeal to an independent tribunal if it's rejected. A Parking Charge Notice comes from a private company. Appeal to the operator, then to POPLA or the IAS. Never just ignore either one.

Council tickets (Penalty Charge Notice)

  1. Informal challenge — within 14 days (21 days for camera-enforced tickets) to keep the discount.
  2. Notice to Owner — if you don't pay or your challenge fails, you have 28 days to make a formal representation.
  3. Independent tribunal — if your representation is rejected, appeal to London Tribunals or the Traffic Penalty Tribunal. It's free and you don't need a lawyer.

Private tickets (Parking Charge Notice)

  1. Appeal to the operator within the time on the notice (often 28 days).
  2. If rejected, appeal to POPLA (BPA members) or the IAS (IPC members). The rejection letter tells you which, with a code.
  3. If the company issues court papers, respond to them. Never ignore a county court claim.

Grounds that often win

  • Signs or road markings were missing, unclear or obscured.
  • You were within a grace period, or loading or unloading where allowed.
  • The machine or app was broken and you have proof.
  • You had a valid permit or Blue Badge displayed.
  • The ticket has errors, or the company didn't follow the rules for postal notices.
  • You weren't the driver, and the keeper liability rules weren't followed (private tickets).

Evidence to gather now

Photos of the signs, markings, your car and the machine, plus receipts, permits, app screenshots and witness details. Take them as soon as possible.

Frequently asked questions

Is a private parking ticket a fine?

No. It's an invoice for an alleged breach of contract. Appeal it properly rather than ignoring it.

Will I lose the discount if I appeal?

For council tickets, challenge within 14 days and most councils re-offer the discount if they reject you.

Who do I appeal to after the council?

London Tribunals or the Traffic Penalty Tribunal. It's free and independent.

Can a private parking company take me to court?

Yes. If you receive court papers, respond within the deadline.
