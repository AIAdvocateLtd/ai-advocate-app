Flight delay compensation in the UK: claim it yourself

Delayed by 3 hours or more, or cancelled at short notice? You could be owed up to £520 per person, and you don't need a claims company that takes a 30% cut.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · UK261 (retained Regulation 261/2004)
Quick answer: if your flight left a UK airport (any airline) or arrived in the UK on a UK/EU airline, and you reached your destination 3+ hours late, you're usually owed £220, £350 or £520 per passenger, unless the airline proves extraordinary circumstances. Claim directly from the airline; it's free.

How much you can claim

  • £220 — flights up to 1,500km
  • £350 — flights 1,500km to 3,500km
  • £520 — flights over 3,500km (may be reduced to £260 if the delay was 3–4 hours)

The delay is measured at arrival, when at least one aircraft door opens.

Cancellations

If your flight was cancelled with less than 14 days' notice, you may be owed compensation depending on the replacement flight offered. You always have the right to a refund or rerouting.

Your right to care during the delay

Whatever the cause, the airline must provide food and drink, a way to communicate, and a hotel and transfers if you're delayed overnight. Keep receipts for reasonable costs you paid yourself.

Extraordinary circumstances

Airlines don't pay compensation for things outside their control, such as severe weather, air traffic control restrictions, security threats or political unrest. Technical faults and staff shortages usually don't count as extraordinary.

How to claim

  1. Note your flight number, date, scheduled and actual arrival times, and the reason you were given.
  2. Use the airline's online claim form or write to them, citing UK261.
  3. If they refuse or ignore you for 8 weeks, escalate to the airline's ADR scheme or the CAA.
  4. As a last resort, use the small claims court.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I get for a delayed flight?

£220, £350 or £520 per passenger depending on distance, if you arrived 3+ hours late.

Which flights are covered?

Any flight leaving the UK, and flights arriving in the UK on a UK or EU airline.

How far back can I claim?

6 years in England and Wales, 5 years in Scotland.

Do I need a claims company?

No. You can claim directly for free and keep 100% of the compensation.
