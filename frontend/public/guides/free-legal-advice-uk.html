Free legal advice in the UK: where to get help without paying

Need a lawyer but worried about the cost? You may not need to pay anything. Here's every free option and which one suits your problem.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · Covers England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland
Quick answer: start with Citizens Advice (any problem), ACAS (work), Shelter (housing) or a Law Centre. If your problem involves eviction, domestic abuse, children being taken into care, discrimination or immigration, check if you can get Legal Aid, which pays for a solicitor. Also check your home insurance for legal expenses cover.

1. Citizens Advice

Free, confidential help on debt, benefits, work, housing, consumer and family problems. Visit a local office, use the online guides, or call the national adviceline.

2. Law Centres

Not-for-profit legal practices with qualified solicitors who can represent you for free, usually on housing, employment, welfare benefits, immigration and discrimination. Find your nearest via the Law Centres Network.

3. Legal Aid

The government pays for a solicitor if your case is in scope and you pass a means test (income and savings) and a merits test (chance of success). See our Legal Aid eligibility guide.

4. Pro bono clinics (LawWorks and Advocate)

Volunteer solicitors and barristers give free advice sessions. Advocate (the Bar's pro bono charity) can find a free barrister for a tribunal or court hearing if you can't afford one.

5. University law clinics

Many universities run free clinics where supervised law students help with letters, tribunal claims and small claims.

6. Specialist helplines

  • ACAS — employment rights and early conciliation
  • Shelter — housing, eviction and homelessness
  • StepChange / National Debtline — debt
  • Equality Advisory Support Service — discrimination
  • Rights of Women — family law and domestic abuse

7. Free first consultation with a solicitor

Many high-street solicitors offer a free 20–30 minute first meeting. Use it to understand your options and the likely cost. See how to find a good solicitor near you.

8. Legal expenses insurance

Check your home, car, travel and packaged bank-account policies. Many include cover for employment, consumer and personal injury disputes worth thousands of pounds.

9. No win, no fee

For personal injury, medical negligence and some employment claims, solicitors may work on a conditional fee agreement. You pay nothing upfront, but a share of any compensation goes to the solicitor if you win.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get free legal advice in the UK?

Citizens Advice, Law Centres, university law clinics, LawWorks pro bono clinics, ACAS, Shelter and Civil Legal Advice if you qualify for Legal Aid.

Can I get a free lawyer?

Yes, through Legal Aid if your problem is in scope and you pass the means and merits tests, or through Law Centres and pro bono schemes.

Does my insurance cover legal fees?

Often. Check home, car and bank-account policies for "legal expenses" cover before paying anyone.

Is AI legal help reliable?

It's a fast way to understand your rights and deadlines and to draft letters, but it isn't regulated legal advice. For complex or high-value matters, speak to a solicitor.
