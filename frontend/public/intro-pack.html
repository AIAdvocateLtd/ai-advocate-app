What is AI Advocate?

A consumer-facing iOS / Android / web app that gives ordinary people structured legal information — and then routes the ones who need real representation to partner law firms.

The product, in one page

  • Lex — our AI legal assistant (powered by Anthropic Claude Sonnet 4.5 / Opus 4.5). Answers questions, drafts letters, analyses contracts and evidence photos.
  • Case Files — encrypted notebooks for each matter (employment, property, immigration, medical, family etc.).
  • Lex Vault — biometric-locked storage for sensitive documents (NDAs, passports, payslips). End-to-end encrypted with the user's PIN — even we cannot read inside.
  • Hearing Recorder — record + transcribe statements, with built-in disclosure that courtroom recording is illegal.
  • Courtroom Practice mode — AI plays opposing counsel, judge, tribunal chair so users can rehearse.
  • Legal Aid Finder — directs users to public funding when eligible.
  • Firm Portal — the bit that matters to you. Encrypted Case Threads between client and your fee-earners.

How leads reach you

The user comes to AI Advocate with a problem. By the time they reach your inbox, they have already articulated it, understood the legal framework, gathered evidence, and decided they want representation.

The funnel

  1. User describes their problem to Lex in plain English (or Polish, Punjabi, Arabic, etc.).
  2. Lex applies the relevant law, drafts letters, surfaces limitation periods, identifies their position.
  3. Lex flags when human counsel is needed — court representation, complex litigation, high-stakes negotiation.
  4. User taps "Find a solicitor" and is matched with a Featured firm in their region + practice area.
  5. You receive a structured brief: jurisdiction, dates, parties, prior Lex analysis, evidence already collected by the user.
  6. Engagement happens inside the app — encrypted Case Thread, file sharing, no email-attachment risk.
What this means in practice: a client who would have wasted 30 minutes of your trainee's time explaining themselves arrives already organised. You spend less time intake-interviewing and more time billing chargeable work.

What you do not have to do

  • Build any technology — the Firm Portal is a web dashboard you log into.
  • Process payments — consumers pay us their subscription; you bill clients directly for retained work.
  • Worry about disclosure — AI Advocate's disclaimers are everywhere; you cannot be implicated for the AI's outputs.

Liability & disclaimers

A frequent question from prospective partner firms: "What if the AI gives bad advice and the user blames us?" Here is exactly how the platform protects you.

The legal architecture

  • AI Advocate provides legal INFORMATION, not legal ADVICE. This is repeated on every screen where Lex outputs content, in our Terms of Service, in every chat message, and in our App Store listing.
  • No solicitor-client relationship is created with AI Advocate or with you until the user formally engages your firm through the Firm Portal.
  • Each Case Thread you accept creates a normal solicitor-client retainer governed by your usual terms — AI Advocate is not a party.
  • The user explicitly acknowledges on signup that "AI Advocate is not a substitute for a qualified lawyer". This consent is logged.
In practice: if a user follows Lex's information and gets it wrong, their claim is against the AI provider (us), governed by our limitation clause. Your firm is only liable for advice you yourself give once retained. This is the same architecture that allows Citizens Advice, MoneySavingExpert, etc. to publish legal information without indemnifying every solicitor.

Your standard engagement letter is unchanged

When a user converts from Lex referral to your retained client, you issue your standard engagement letter on your firm's letterhead. AI Advocate disappears from the relationship at that point. Our involvement is limited to providing the encrypted channel for client communications, and only if you wish to use it.

Our compliance posture

  • ICO-registered data controller (Reg ZC158457) — verifiable at ico.org.uk.
  • UK-GDPR compliant with documented DPIA for SOS Live Tracking, Vault, audio, and AI profiling.
  • AES-GCM zero-knowledge encryption for the Lex Vault.
  • Professional indemnity & cyber liability cover being arranged for the App Store launch window.

The "Founding Partner" question

Some firms ask for app-wide exclusivity. Honest counsel: this is rarely the right structure for either side. Here are three forms of partner prominence that work in practice.

OptionWhat it coversIndicative monthly fee
Geographic LeadSole "recommended local solicitor" for a city / county / postcode cluster.£1,500 – £5,000
Practice-Area LeadSole "recommended firm" for one vertical (e.g. Employment, Property) in a given region.£2,500 – £8,000
National Founding PartnerLogo lockup in-app + App Store listing co-credit + 12-month limited exclusivity in your speciality.By negotiation

Why we do not offer full app-wide exclusivity

  • It harms users — and the App Store reviews us out of existence if we route every user to one firm.
  • Apple Guideline 4.2 (Substantive Value) can be triggered for single-merchant shopfront apps.
  • SRA Code of Conduct rule 5.1 requires that exclusive referrals are disclosed in a way that becomes practically untenable.
  • It caps our and your growth — investors will not back a one-firm-tied product.
What we will agree to: a time-limited (6–12 month) regional or practice-area first-look arrangement, with right of first refusal on incoming leads in your defined territory. This gives a real commercial moat without the structural problems above.

Sponsorship slot (new for v1.x)

We are reserving one in-app sponsorship slot — a discreet "In partnership with [Firm Name]" footer that appears across the consumer app. Pricing on application. Available to one firm only, renewable annually.
